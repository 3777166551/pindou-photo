Privacy Policy 隐私政策

App: Photo → Beads(照片变拼豆 / PindouPhoto)  ·  Effective / 生效日期: 2026-09-04

1. We collect nothing / 中文:我们不收集任何数据

This app has no network permission at all. It cannot connect to the internet, so it cannot send, upload, or share any of your data — with us or with anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no trackers.

本 APP 不申请任何网络权限,技术上无法联网,因此不可能向任何服务器 发送、上传或分享你的数据。没有账号体系、没有统计分析、没有广告 SDK、没有任何跟踪器。

2. Permissions / 权限说明

  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — requested only on Android 9 or older (maxSdkVersion=28), solely to save exported pattern images into the public Pictures folder. On Android 10+ this permission is never requested (MediaStore needs none).
  • 唯一权限为 WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE,且仅限 Android 9 及以下 (maxSdkVersion=28)用于把导出的图纸图片保存到相册 Pictures 目录; Android 10+ 完全不使用该权限(走 MediaStore,无需权限)。

3. Where your data lives / 数据存在哪里

Everything you create — projects, palettes, bead inventory, check-in records — is stored only on your device in the app's private folder. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Files you export (PNG / PDF / JSON) are saved where you choose and shared only when you choose to share them.

你创建的一切(项目存档、自定义色板、豆仓库存、打卡记录)都只保存在你设备上的 应用私有目录中,卸载即全部删除。你导出的文件(PNG / PDF / JSON)保存在你选择的位置, 只有你主动操作时才会分享出去。

4. Offline AI models / 离线 AI 模型

The background-removal (U²-Net) and style-transfer (AnimeGANv3) features run on your device via ONNX Runtime. Images never leave the phone.

去背景(U²-Net)与风格化(AnimeGANv3)功能通过 ONNX Runtime 完全在手机本地推理,图片不会离开设备。

5. Children / 未成年人

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Note: ironing finished bead projects requires adult supervision.

本 APP 不收集任何用户(包括儿童)的数据。请注意:熨烫成品环节需要成人陪同。

6. Contact / 联系方式

Source code (AGPL-3.0): github.com/3777166551/pindou-photo — open an issue for any privacy question.

开源地址(AGPL-3.0):github.com/3777166551/pindou-photo,任何隐私问题请提 Issue。

Pattern colors, codes, quantities and costs are algorithmic estimates — always compare with the official brand color chart and real beads before ordering. The software is provided "as is" under AGPL-3.0, without warranty of any kind.

图纸颜色、色号、用量与成本均为算法估算值,下单前请以各品牌官方色卡与实物为准。 本软件按"现状"以 AGPL-3.0 协议提供,不附带任何形式的担保。
