Turn any photo into a perler/fuse bead pattern — fully offline. Snap your cat, your drawing or your avatar and get a beading chart plus a shopping list. Candy-sticker UI designed for beaders.

✨ Features
• Photo → chart: 4 universal palettes (24/48/90/120 colors) mapped to Artkal / MARD / Perler / Hama / Nabbi color codes
• Realistic / abstract / line-art styles, k-means quantization + Floyd–Steinberg dithering, round boards supported
• Quality modes: clean outlines / noise cleanup / precise colors (CIEDE2000); color-limit merging (12/18/26/40)
• On-device background removal (built-in U²-Net model, no network), watermark removal, and photo-of-finished-piece → chart recovery
• Snap an old pattern: automatic grid detection; PDF charts can be imported too
• Built-in template library: 277 patterns in 8 categories (based on Fluent Emoji, MIT)
• Bead list: counts, share, weight, cost, pack conversion (≈N packs of 1000), inventory and substitution suggestions
• Beading assistant: per-color locating, tap/swipe marking, board/row guidance, check-in calendar, keep-screen-on
• 3D preview and stacked multi-layer compositions with merged bead lists
• Projector alignment: pin the current color onto your real pegboard with 4-point calibration
• AR try-on, cross-stitch export (DMC 454 floss), GIF export, full backup/restore, open charts from WeChat/QQ

🔒 Privacy: the app requests NO internet permission — no network, no uploads, no ads, no tracking.
